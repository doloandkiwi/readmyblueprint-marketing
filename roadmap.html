● Roadmap

What's actually being considered next.

This isn't a voting board or a marketing wishlist — it's an honest status of what's real, what's a maybe, and what's deliberately not planned right now, and why. See something missing you'd actually pay for? Tell us directly.

Actively considering

AI-agent (MCP) tooling expansion

The read-only MCP server just shipped — considering what else would be useful to expose the same way.

Re-enabling in-app AI chat

Already built, currently off to avoid an unbounded per-question AI cost pre-revenue. Likely comes back in a capped free tier.

Real, but later

Multi-seat / team accounts

Today it's one account per person. Real multi-user support gets built once an actual team asks for it.

Regional material/labor cost data

A genuinely large feature with real ongoing data costs — worth it once there's revenue to support it.

More material categories

Expanding beyond steel/wood framing, drywall, plywood, insulation, and CMU is a real product decision, not a content task.

Not planned right now

Native iOS/Android/desktop apps

The installable web app (PWA) already covers most of the real benefit for far less overhead.

Live "book a demo" calls

Runs against the whole point of a $15, no-sales-call product.

Scanned/raster PDF support

There's no real geometry in a scanned image to measure — this would mean guessing, which is the one thing this tool refuses to do.
