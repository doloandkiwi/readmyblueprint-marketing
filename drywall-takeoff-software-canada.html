What works the same on a Canadian set

The job is the same as anywhere: find the wall-type legend, measure each partition, group by wall type, convert length and height to board, studs and track. The step-by-step is on how to do a drywall takeoff from PDF plans. What changes on a Canadian set is the units and the names on the legend.

  • Digital PDF only. It needs a PDF exported from CAD or Revit. Scanned or photographed sheets cannot be measured. Scanned plans are counted too: upload them or send them, and the counts come back the same way. See vector vs. scanned PDFs.
  • Counts by wall type, traced to the wall. Each result carries a confidence label, and anything the legend does not settle is flagged for you.
  • CSV and Excel export, with the label column included, so you can convert units in your own sheet.

The metric limit, stated plainly

ReadMyBlueprint reports lengths in feet and areas in square feet. A metre-based price list needs a conversion step after export. We have not published a test on a metric-scale plan set, and we have not checked how every metric scale ratio (1:50, 1:100 and so on) reads. Treat the first upload as a check: compare the scale it reports and one wall length against a dimension printed on the sheet before you trust any total. Calibration is covered in why PDF plan measurements are off.

The wall type legend is where metric sets differ most. Wall heights, stud sizes and board thickness are written in millimetres, often with no unit symbol. Read the legend in mm, then convert once, in one place, with a column that shows both numbers. Do not convert wall by wall.

Metric to imperial: the conversions you need

These are plain arithmetic, not a manufacturer spec. 1 ft = 304.8 mm. 1 m = 3.2808 ft. 1 m² = 10.764 sq ft.

The 38 x 89 and 38 x 140 mm sizes are the metric names for the 2x4 and 2x6 (checked against the dimensional lumber table on Wikipedia, Nov 9, 2026). For steel studs, the metric web depths are on metal stud sizes and gauges. Not checked: which sheet lengths a given Canadian supplier stocks. Ask your supplier before you round up to whole sheets.

Worked example, made-up numbers, in metric

One partition, 12 m long, 2700 mm high, board on both sides. Board area is 12 x 2.7 x 2 = 64.8 m². Divided by 2.977 m² per 1220 x 2440 mm sheet, that is 21.8 sheets before waste. Studs at 400 mm centres: 12 000 / 400 = 30 spaces, so 31 studs, plus extras at ends, corners and openings. Track is two runs of 12 m, top and bottom. The software does this per wall type across the whole set. Add waste for your own cutting pattern: see the drywall waste factor page.

Terms you will see on a Canadian legend

  • Gypsum board, GWB, drywall. Same material. The legend usually says gypsum board or GWB; the crew says drywall. Plasterboard is a third name for it.
  • Type X, fire-rated board. Fire-rated walls carry a heavier or doubled board layer. Count them as their own wall type. See fire-rated wall takeoff.
  • Steel studs and track. Written by web depth in mm plus a thickness code. Take the legend's words for it; this page does not decode manufacturer codes.
  • RCP. Reflected ceiling plan. ReadMyBlueprint sets RCP sheets aside so walls are not counted twice. Ceilings come off by hand: ceiling drywall takeoff.

Proof, with its limits

On a real 21-page apartment set (Freeport Square, a US plan set in feet and inches), 68.9% of walls auto-matched to the plan's own wall legend and 99.8% resolved after one "Confirm all". That is one plan set, not a promise about yours, and it was not a metric set. The legend shared a sheet with the door schedule, so the extracted text came out scrambled and the rest was flagged for a person instead of guessed. Read the full test, accuracy and how we count.

Where it fits for a Canadian estimator

Use it for the slow, mechanical part: measuring every partition and sorting it by wall type. You still own the final number: confirm flagged walls, apply your waste and convert units. It does not currently measure paint or flooring, and ceilings are by hand. For fit-out sets, see tenant improvement drywall takeoff.

FAQ

Does ReadMyBlueprint work with metric drawings?

It reads digital PDF plan sets and reports quantities in feet and square feet, not metres. We have not published a test on a metric-scale set, so check the scale it reports against a printed dimension on your first upload, then convert in Excel or CSV.

What is a 38 x 89 mm stud?

It is the metric name for a 2x4, an actual size of 1 1/2 x 3 1/2 inches. A 38 x 140 mm stud is a 2x6, 1 1/2 x 5 1/2 inches.

How many sheets of drywall is a 12 m wall at 2700 mm high?

With board on both sides, 12 x 2.7 x 2 = 64.8 square metres. A 1220 x 2440 mm sheet covers about 2.977 square metres, so about 21.8 sheets before waste.

Can it read scanned drawings?

No. It needs a digital PDF exported from design software. Scanned or photographed sheets have no drawing data to measure.

Does it do ceilings, flooring or paint?

No. It counts walls by wall type from the floor plans. Ceilings, flooring and paint are by hand.
