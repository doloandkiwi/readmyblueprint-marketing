How do I count drywall from a PDF?

By hand, measure each wall's length at the printed scale, multiply by height, add both faces and divide by the board area. That works but is slow on a big set. With ReadMyBlueprint you upload the PDF and it does the measuring by wall type, so your work is checking the flagged walls instead.

How many sheets of drywall do I need from linear feet of wall?

Multiply wall length by wall height to get one face in square feet, double it for two faces, then divide by the sheet area, which is 32 for a 4x8 sheet. Add your waste on top. The software does this per wall type, so a mixed set does not need one formula for every wall.

Many small drywall shops use a mix: paper plans and a scale ruler, spreadsheets, PDF measuring tools, or a takeoff service. Software that reads the plan for you is the newer option. I only have scanned plans. Can I still get a drywall takeoff?

Yes. Digital PDFs are measured instantly, and scanned plans are counted too, usually the same day. Scans take longer because the lines and text are pictures, so more items may be flagged for you to confirm. Upload the scan you have and check the flagged walls against the sheet.

How long does a drywall takeoff take with software?

A digital PDF is measured instantly. Scanned plans are usually counted the same day, and always within 24 hours. Your own time after that goes to confirming the flagged walls and applying waste. How long that takes depends on how many walls are flagged and how clean the drawings are.

Does drywall takeoff software count both sides of a wall?

It depends on how the legend and wall type are drawn, so check this on your first set. ReadMyBlueprint returns board by wall type from the legend, and every number is traced to its wall so you can see what was counted. If a wall has different board on each face, confirm that line before you bid.

Can software tell Type X from regular drywall?

Only if the plan says so. ReadMyBlueprint reads board from the wall-type legend, so a Type X or fire-rated layer shows up when the legend lists it. If the legend is silent or the note is on another sheet, the wall is flagged for you to confirm. Check the fire-rated walls first.

Can I export a drywall takeoff to Excel?

Yes. You can export to Excel or CSV, or to a priced quote. The export keeps the counts by wall type so you can apply your own waste and labor in your own bid sheet. Our CSV is a plain table, so it drops into most estimating spreadsheets.

Yes. Bluebeam Revu is a PDF markup tool with measurement features, and many estimators use it for takeoff. You click or draw each measurement yourself. What will drywall takeoff software not count?

ReadMyBlueprint does not count doors, windows, electrical, plumbing, mechanical, roofing, fasteners, or tape and mud. It covers walls by wall type: studs, track, drywall and board sheets, insulation, plywood and sheathing, and CMU block. Anything outside that list you still take off and price yourself.

What happens if the plan has no wall type legend?

The walls it cannot match to a type are flagged for you to confirm instead of guessed. A plan with no legend means the software has nothing to read the board and stud spec from, so expect more flagged items and more of your own checking. Our blog covers a real case with no legend.

How do I check a drywall takeoff on a big apartment set?

Start with the flagged items, then spot-check one wall type by hand and click through to the walls behind its total. Compare that wall type against the sheet and the legend. If your hand count and the app disagree, the trace shows which wall caused it. Then apply your waste and bid.
