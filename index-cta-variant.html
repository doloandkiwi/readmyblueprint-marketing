What you get back

  • Studs and track by wall type, in LF and piece count.
  • Drywall sheets for both sides of every wall, with Type X and moisture-resistant board on their own lines.
  • Insulation by wall type, and CMU block counts by block size.
  • A list you can export to Excel or CSV and drop into your bid.
  • A flag on every wall we're not sure about, so you check it instead of trusting a blind number.

What to expect: your first plan set is counted by three independent automated passes, each checked against the others, before you see it. It usually comes back the same day, always within 24 hours. Counts are an assisted takeoff. You confirm them before they go in a bid.
