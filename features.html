What ReadMyBlueprint does, and what it doesn't.

Upload a digital plan set and get studs, track, drywall, insulation and block counts back, every number traced to the wall it came from, anything uncertain flagged for you.

Reading the plan set

  • Upload the whole PDF as-is. No tracing, no clicking walls.
  • It starts scanning every page automatically: wall geometry, the printed scale and the wall-type text. The 21-page Freeport Square set took about a minute.
  • Ceiling plans, elevations and details redraw walls already on the floor plans. It sets those sheets aside so it won't count the same wall twice, and tells you which ones.
  • A 100+ page plan set can take several minutes. Every plan set you upload stays listed under "Your projects."
Watch the tour →Help Center walkthrough →

Wall measurement & wall-type matching

  • Every wall is measured directly from the vector data in your plan set: its length and drawn thickness, calibrated to the sheet's own printed scale.
  • That thickness is checked against the wall-type legend already drawn on your plan set.
  • One legend match: matched. More than one: ambiguous, with every candidate shown. None: needs confirmation. Never silently assigned a best guess.
  • Flagged walls get a suggested type; you check and confirm them, in one click with "Confirm all", with no re-upload. In the tour run, resolved walls went from 68.9% to 99.8%.

Confidence labels & flags

  • Every auto-detected result is labeled matched, ambiguous, or needs confirmation. Never left unmarked.
  • Rows the tool can't identify show as SPEC UNKNOWN and are left out of the totals until you confirm them.
  • You can override any flagged result. Your confirmation is recorded as yours, not silently reclassified as "matched."
  • An unconfirmed row stays flagged in your export. It is never quietly turned into a confident-looking number.

Materials output per trade

  • One results table with a column per material: CMU, Metal, Lumber, Drywall, Insulation.
  • Drywall: a sheet count you can trace back to the wall. Metal framing: steel stud and track count tied to the gauge on your own legend.
  • Wood framing: wood stud and plywood counts from the actual wall. Insulation: coverage tied to the wall cavity. CMU: a block count from real wall area.
  • In the tour run, the 2x6 walls came back as 3,008 LF, 2,264 studs, 605 track, 1,131 drywall sheets and 208 bags of insulation, and 8,108 CMU blocks for the 10.5-inch walls.

Export CSV / Excel

  • "Export these results (CSV)" turns the results table into a spreadsheet you can open in Excel and drop into a bid.
  • Every export includes the label column, so anyone reviewing the file sees what was verified and what wasn't.
  • Each material lists the sheets it was counted on, so you can check any line against the drawing before it goes in a bid.
  • Read-only API keys are available from your account, scoped to your own documents and material summaries.

Supported files & limits

  • Needs a real digital plan file exported from design software such as AutoCAD or Revit.
  • A scanned or photographed page has no drawing data to measure, so the tool says it can't measure that page rather than guess. If only some pages are scanned, the digital pages still work.
  • Wall types are checked against the legend on your own plan set. If the plan set has no legend, every wall is still measured, but results mostly come back as needs confirmation.
  • Solo Estimator includes 10 plan sets a month; Small Firm is unlimited. See pricing.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't read scanned, photographed or flat-image PDFs.
  • It doesn't guess a wall's material to make a number look complete.
  • It doesn't auto-detect doors, windows, pipe, fixtures or other one-off items. Add those with a click on the drawing or "Manual count"; they go into the same takeoff.
  • It doesn't publish a single blended accuracy percentage. Results are labeled row by row, and the tested numbers are on the accuracy page.
  • The API can't upload, edit or delete anything. It is read-only.
  • It is assisted takeoff: you verify every bid before it goes out.
