Short answer: Quantity takeoff software measures lengths, areas and counts from digital plans so you can turn them into a material list. Most tools have you trace every item. What it measures

The three things quantity takeoff software measures

Every quantity on a wall-trade bid comes from one of three measurements. The software's job is to get them off the sheet without you scaling them by hand.

MeasurementWall-trade examplesTurns into
Linear (LF)Partition runs by wall type, top and bottom track, control joints, corner beadStuds, track, bead, sealant
Area (sq ft)Wall faces by board layer, ceiling areas, insulated cavity areaBoard sheets, batts or bags, mud and tape
Count (EA)Door and window openings, headers, blocking locationsHeader pieces, jamb studs, deductions from board area
Worked numbers

From one wall type to a quantity list

Worked example: 200 LF of one partition type, 10 ft tall, 3-5/8 in. metal studs 16 in. on center, one layer of 5/8 in. Type X each side, sound batts in the cavity.

That is one wall type. A 20-sheet set can carry 10 or more types, each with its own stud size, spacing, board layers and fire rating, so the slow part is not the math. It is finding every run of every type and keeping them apart. ReadMyBlueprint measures the runs from the PDF, matches each wall to its row in your legend and returns this table per wall type. Questions

Common questions

What is quantity takeoff software?

Quantity takeoff software measures lengths, areas and counts from digital plans so an estimator can turn them into material quantities. For wall trades that means partition runs by wall type, wall face area by board layer and opening counts, which become studs, track, board sheets and insulation.

What is the difference between a quantity takeoff and a material takeoff?

A quantity takeoff measures the plan: linear feet, square feet and counts. A material takeoff converts those measurements into orderable items such as 151 studs, 400 LF of track and 100 sheets of board. ReadMyBlueprint returns both, by wall type.

Can AI do a quantity takeoff?

AI can propose most of it, but it is assisted, not fully automatic. ReadMyBlueprint measures walls from the PDF and matches them to your legend with three independent automated passes, each checked against the others, and flags anything it can't match for you to confirm. Our one published test: Freeport Square (21 pages), 68.9% of walls were auto-matched and 99.8% after one 'Confirm all.'

Does quantity takeoff software work on scanned PDFs?

Many manual tools let you trace a scanned sheet by hand. ReadMyBlueprint reads them as well. Scanned plans are counted too.
