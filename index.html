How it works

From a plan set to a bid-ready count, in three steps.

No special file format, no manual setup beyond a one-time scale check. It works directly from the digital plan PDF your architect or engineer already sent you — whether you're a homeowner doing one small remodel or an estimating team running dozens of sheets a week. No CAD or estimating background required.

1

Upload your plan set

Drop in the full PDF — one sheet or two hundred. ReadMyBlueprint reads the sheet's own printed scale directly off the drawing, so there's no manual calibration step for most projects.

2

It reads the drawing, not just the shapes

ReadMyBlueprint measures every wall, then cross-references the real wall-type notes and legends printed on your sheets to identify the exact stud size, gauge, and spacing — automatically.

3

Review, confirm, export

Get a plain-English dashboard: studs, track, drywall sheets, insulation bags, CMU blocks. Confirm anything flagged, then export straight to a bid-ready CSV or Excel sheet.

Does this work with scanned or flat-image PDFs?

No. ReadMyBlueprint works with digital plan files exported directly from design software — AutoCAD, Revit, or similar. A scanned or photographed plan has no real drawing data for it to measure, so the tool will tell you it can't measure those pages rather than guess. If your plan set is a mix of real digital sheets and scanned ones (common on renovation/repair projects that reference older drawings), the digital sheets still work fine.
