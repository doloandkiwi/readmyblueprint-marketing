How it works

From a plan set to a bid-ready count, in three steps.

No special file format, no manual setup beyond a one-time scale check. It works directly from the vector PDF your architect or engineer already sent you.

1

Upload your plan set

Drop in the full PDF — one sheet or two hundred. ReadMyBlueprint reads the sheet's own printed scale directly off the drawing, so there's no manual calibration step for most projects.

2

It reads the drawing, not just the shapes

ReadMyBlueprint measures every wall, then cross-references the real wall-type notes and legends printed on your sheets to identify the exact stud size, gauge, and spacing — automatically.

3

Review, confirm, export

Get a plain-English dashboard: studs, track, drywall sheets, insulation bags, CMU blocks. Confirm anything flagged, then export straight to a bid-ready CSV or Excel sheet.

Why it's different

Every other tool claims accuracy. We show our work.

No independent benchmark exists for any takeoff AI on the market. Instead of asking you to trust a number, ReadMyBlueprint labels every single one — so you always know exactly where to spend your two minutes of double-checking.

AmbiguousMore than one material could explain this wall's thickness — ReadMyBlueprint tells you exactly which ones, instead of silently picking one.
