Does this work with scanned or flat-image PDFs?

No. ReadMyBlueprint works with digital plan files exported directly from design software — AutoCAD, Revit, or similar. A scanned or photographed plan has no real drawing data for it to measure, so the tool will tell you it can't measure those pages rather than guess. If your plan set is a mix of real digital sheets and scanned ones (common on renovation/repair projects that reference older drawings), the digital sheets still work fine.
