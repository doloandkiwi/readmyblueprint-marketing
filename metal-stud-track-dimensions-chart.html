Metal Stud and Track Dimensions Chart: Web, Flange and Return Lip

By The ReadMyBlueprint Team · Nov 25, 2026 · Updated Nov 25, 2026

A standard interior steel stud has a 3-5/8 inch web, a 1-1/4 inch flange and a 1/4 inch return lip, in 15 to 33 mil steel, and its matching track has the same web depth with legs of 1-1/4 inch or longer and no return lip. Structural studs widen the options: flanges of 1-3/8 to 3 inches, return lips of 3/8 to 5/8 inch, and thicknesses from 33 to 97 mil.

The charts below use ClarkDietrich's published product data (the catalog effective 05/12/26 and the SubmittalPro identifier sheet effective 06/01/24) and CEMCO's Viper-X catalog (effective 02/03/2026). For gauge and mil conversion see the metal stud gauge chart; for lengths and finished wall thickness see metal stud sizes and dimensions.

How to read a stud or track part number

Part numbers follow the AISI nomenclature that ClarkDietrich, a member of the Steel Framing Industry Association (SFIA), uses on its products: web depth in hundredths of an inch, a letter for the member type, flange in hundredths of an inch, then a hyphen and the minimum delivered thickness in mils. 362S125-33 is a 3.625 inch web, a stud, a 1.25 inch flange and 33 mil steel. 362T125-33 is the matching track. The letters are S for stud or joist, T for track, U for U-channel and F for furring channel, and ClarkDietrich's drywall lines add a P prefix (PDS for ProSTUD, PDT for ProTRAK).

ClarkDietrich's 18 mil stud submittal lists a 0.325 inch stiffening lip where its 15, 30 and 33 mil sheets list 0.250 inch, so confirm the lip on the exact product if it matters.

CEMCO Viper-X: a different flange and lip

Not every manufacturer uses the 1-1/4 inch flange. CEMCO's Viper-X stud is a proprietary profile with a 1-7/16 inch flange and a 3/8 inch return lip, and CEMCO's catalog notes that its design stiffening lip is 3/8 inch for all studs. Its track has 1-1/4, 1-1/2 and 2 inch legs.

Where the dimensions show up in a takeoff

  • The legend often leaves out the flange. A wall type legend normally states depth and gauge, for example 3-5/8 inch 20 GA at 16 inch on center. The flange and lip come from the product the supplier ships, so the same legend line can be priced as 1-1/4 inch or 1-5/8 inch flange stock.
  • Substituting between makers is not automatic. ClarkDietrich's own catalog warns that studs of the same size and thickness from different manufacturers are not always interchangeable until an engineer compares the section properties. A Viper-X flange is not a ProSTUD flange.
  • Track follows the wall, not the stud flange. Order track at the stud's web depth and the thickness the wall calls for, and use the leg length the head-of-wall detail needs. ClarkDietrich's head-of-wall tables, for example, use a 2-1/2 inch leg MaxTRAK.
  • Group by depth and mil. Same depth in two thicknesses is two line items.

Frequently asked questions

What are the dimensions of a 3-5/8 inch metal stud?

A 3-5/8 inch (3.625 in., 362) drywall stud from ClarkDietrich has a 1-1/4 inch flange and a 1/4 inch return lip, in 15, 18, 30 or 33 mil steel. Structural 3-5/8 inch studs come with 1-3/8, 1-5/8, 2 or 2-1/2 inch flanges in 33 to 97 mil.

What is the flange width of a metal stud?

It depends on the series. ClarkDietrich drywall studs have a 1-1/4 inch flange. Its structural studs have 1-3/8, 1-5/8, 2, 2-1/2 or 3 inch flanges depending on web depth. CEMCO's Viper-X stud has a 1-7/16 inch flange.

What is the return lip on a metal stud?

The return lip is the short edge folded back from the flange to stiffen it. ClarkDietrich lists 1/4 inch for the 1-1/4 inch drywall flange, 3/8 inch for 1-3/8, 1/2 inch for 1-5/8 and 5/8 inch for 2, 2-1/2 and 3 inch flanges. CEMCO's Viper-X stud is 3/8 inch.

Does metal track have a return lip?

No. Track is a U-shape with two legs and no return lip. ClarkDietrich's ProTRAK legs are 1-1/4, 1-1/2, 2, 2-1/2 or 3 inches, and CEMCO's Viper-X track legs are 1-1/4, 1-1/2 or 2 inches.

What does 362S125-33 mean?

The 362 is a 3.625 inch web depth, S is a stud, 125 is a 1.25 inch flange and 33 is 33 mil (0.033 inch) minimum thickness. Replace the S with T for the matching track, 362T125-33.

How thick is a 25 gauge, 20 gauge or 18 gauge stud?

ClarkDietrich lists its 25 gauge drywall stud at 15 mil (0.0150 inch minimum), 20 gauge drywall at 30 mil (0.0296 inch), 20 gauge structural at 33 mil (0.0329 inch) and 18 gauge structural at 43 mil (0.0428 inch). Gauge is a reference and the mil number is what gets ordered.
