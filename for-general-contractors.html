● For general contractors

An independent number, when you can't just take a sub's word for it.

Cross-check a subcontractor's submitted material count against an independent measurement of the same plan set, or turn around your own bid fast when an opportunity needs an answer today, not next week.

The problem this solves

You're accountable for numbers you didn't produce yourself.

When a sub hands you a material count, you're trusting their math without an easy way to verify it — and a bad number from one trade can wipe out your margin on the whole job. Running a full independent takeoff yourself usually isn't practical when you're managing the project, not estimating it full-time.

ReadMyBlueprint gives you a fast, independent second opinion: upload the same plan set, get a real measured count back, and compare it against what you were handed — without needing to be an estimator yourself, and without the turnaround time of hiring one for a spot-check.

Where this fits your workflow

A fast, independent check — not a full estimating department.

FAQ

Questions GCs actually ask.

Can I use this to double-check a sub's material count?
Yes — upload the same plan set your sub bid from and compare their numbers against ReadMyBlueprint's independent measurement. A big mismatch is worth a conversation before you sign off.

No — one account, upload as many plan sets as your plan allows (or pay per upload for occasional use).
Can multiple people on my team use one account?
Not yet — accounts are single-user today. If you need shared team access, email sales and we'll talk about what you actually need.
How fast can I get results on a rush bid?
Minutes from upload to a full labeled material count, not hours — that's the specific problem this solves for a time-sensitive opportunity.
