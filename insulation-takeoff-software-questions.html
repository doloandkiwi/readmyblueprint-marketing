Is there software that calculates insulation from a PDF plan?

Yes. Upload a PDF plan set to ReadMyBlueprint and it returns insulation by wall type, read from the plan's wall-type legend and printed scale. Each total traces to the walls it came from, and anything uncertain is flagged for you to confirm. It runs in your browser.

Can AI do an insulation takeoff?

AI can do the measuring and matching, and you verify. ReadMyBlueprint reads walls and the legend with three independent automated passes, each checked against the others, and flags what it is unsure about. It is assisted takeoff: confirm flagged walls and apply your own waste before bidding.

How do I count insulation from a PDF plan?

By hand, measure each insulated wall's length at the printed scale, and multiply by height for square feet. That is slow across a full set. With ReadMyBlueprint you upload the PDF and get insulation by wall type to check instead.

How do I figure insulation square footage from wall length?

Multiply the wall length by the wall height for the area of one wall, then add the walls that carry the same insulation. Divide by the coverage per bag, roll or batt, and add waste. The software does this per wall type, using what the plan's legend says.

What do small insulation contractors use for takeoff?

Small shops commonly use scale rulers, spreadsheets, PDF measuring tools or a takeoff service. We do not have survey data on market share, so we will not quote a percentage. Software that reads the plan and returns counts is the newer option, and you can test it free.

Does any software read insulation from the wall type legend?

Yes. ReadMyBlueprint reads the plan's own wall-type legend and matches each wall on the sheets to its type, so insulation comes back by wall type. If the legend does not call out insulation for a wall, there is nothing to read, and the wall is flagged for you.

I only have scanned plans. Can I get an insulation takeoff?

Yes. Digital PDFs are measured instantly, and scanned plans are counted too, usually the same day. Scans are harder to read, so expect more flagged items to confirm. Upload the scan you have and check the flags first. Because scans are pictures rather than vector lines, spend your checking time on flagged walls and on any wall type you cannot read on the sheet yourself.

How long does an insulation takeoff take with software?

A digital PDF is measured instantly. Scanned plans are usually counted the same day and always within 24 hours. After that, your time goes to confirming flagged walls and applying waste, which depends on how many are flagged and how clear the drawings are.

Does it separate insulation by wall type?

Yes. ReadMyBlueprint returns insulation grouped by wall type, so exterior, demising and interior walls with different insulation show on their own lines when the legend distinguishes them. Every total is traced to its walls, so you can check a line against the sheet.

Can it take off spray foam or only batts?

It returns insulation quantity by wall type from the legend. Whether that quantity suits spray foam, batts or board depends on what the legend specifies, and thickness and product are for you to confirm. Check the first set against your own method. Our spray foam versus batt page covers the differences.

Does it count ceiling and attic insulation?

We do not list ceilings or attics as covered. ReadMyBlueprint returns insulation by wall type, and it does not count roofing. Take off ceiling and attic insulation yourself from the right sheets, and check the output on your first plan set to see exactly what was included.

What will insulation takeoff software not count?

ReadMyBlueprint does not count doors, windows, electrical, plumbing, mechanical, roofing, fasteners, or tape and mud. It covers wall material by wall type: studs, track, drywall and board sheets, insulation, plywood and sheathing, and CMU block. Take off and price anything else yourself.

Can I export an insulation takeoff to Excel?

Yes. Export to Excel or CSV, or to a priced quote. The export keeps counts by wall type so you can apply your waste and prices in your own sheet. The CSV is a plain table that drops into most estimating spreadsheets.

Can I do an insulation takeoff in Bluebeam?

Yes. Bluebeam Revu is a PDF markup tool with measurement features, and many estimators use it for takeoff by clicking each wall. Check their site for current features and pricing. ReadMyBlueprint instead reads the plan and returns counts by wall type for you to verify.
