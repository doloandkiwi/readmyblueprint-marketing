Can takeoff software read scanned residential plans?

By The ReadMyBlueprint Team · Nov 10, 2026 · Updated Nov 10, 2026

Yes, with one extra step. ReadMyBlueprint measures digital (vector) PDFs, and a scanned or photographed house plan has no drawing data to measure, so the software tells you it cannot measure those pages instead of guessing. Ask the designer for the original PDF export first. If only scans exist, send them on the send-plans page and we count them with three independent automated passes. First plan set free, no card.

Why scans fail

A digital PDF from CAD stores each wall as lines with real coordinates. A scan stores a picture of paper. There is nothing for software to measure, and tracing the picture needs a scale you set by hand on every sheet. That is why vector vs. scanned PDFs matters before anything else on a residential job.

Is yours actually scanned? A 30-second check

  1. Open the PDF and zoom to 800% on a wall corner. Vector lines stay sharp. A scan turns to pixels and gray fuzz.
  2. Try to select a room label with the text cursor. Text you can select is digital. A scan selects nothing, or the whole page as one block.
  3. Check the file size. A 10-sheet house set of a few MB is usually vector. A set of 40 MB or more for 10 sheets is usually scanned images.

A mixed set is common: digital floor plans with scanned engineer pages added. The digital sheets still work and the scanned ones are skipped.

Routes that work, in order

  • Ask for the original export. Designers and architects almost always have the CAD PDF. Asking costs one email and turns the job into a normal takeoff.
  • Ask for a plot to PDF, not a print. Some portals hand out a flattened image PDF. Ask for a "vector PDF" or "plot to PDF".
  • Send the scans to us. The send plans page says to send scanned plans there and we will do the counts. Scanned sets are counted with three independent automated passes. We have not published a test result for scanned sets.
  • Hand takeoff. For one small house, a scale rule and the drywall calculator may be quicker than any tool.

What residential sets look like once they are digital

House plans usually have no wall-type legend. Walls are one or two types noted in general notes, such as 2x4 interior and 2x6 exterior. Our real test set (Freeport Square) was an apartment set with a legend, so it does not prove how residential sets behave. For how the software handles a set with no legend, read taking off walls with no wall-type legend. Basements and additions are covered in basement finishing takeoff and how to take off wood framing from a PDF floor plan.

Proof, with its limits. On a real 21-page apartment set (Freeport Square), 68.9% of wall length matched automatically to the plan's own wall legend and 99.8% resolved after one "Confirm all". That is one plan set, not a promise about yours. It was a digital set. We have no published result on scanned residential plans.

What you still do by hand

Ceilings, flooring and paint. Confirm the flagged walls, because every count should be checked before it goes into a bid. Apply your own waste: drywall waste factor.

FAQ

Can takeoff software read scanned residential plans?

Not directly. ReadMyBlueprint needs a digital vector PDF. A scan is a picture with no drawing data, so the software says it cannot measure those pages rather than guess.

How do I tell if a PDF is scanned?

Zoom to 800% on a wall corner. Vector lines stay sharp and a scan turns to pixels. Also try selecting a room label with the text cursor: selectable text means digital.

What if I only have scanned house plans?

Ask the designer for the original CAD PDF first. If none exists, send the scans on the send-plans page and we count them with three independent automated passes. We have not published a test for scanned sets.

Do residential plans have a wall-type legend?

Usually not. Walls are noted in general notes, such as 2x4 interior and 2x6 exterior. Our published test set had a legend, so it does not cover how residential sets behave.

Does a mixed scanned and digital set work?

The digital sheets are measured and the scanned ones are skipped.
