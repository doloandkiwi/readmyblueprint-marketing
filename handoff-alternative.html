Handoff alternative for commercial trade takeoffs

They estimate remodels from a prompt; we count your actual plan set. An honest look at where Handoff fits, and where a plan-set takeoff tool fits better.

The short version

They estimate remodels from a prompt. We count your actual plan set.

Handoff (handoff.ai) is an AI estimating and project-management app for residential remodelers and builders. You describe a job by text, voice or photo and it produces a priced estimate and proposal, alongside CRM, invoicing and a client portal. ReadMyBlueprint does a narrower job: it reads the real plan set and counts walls, metal and wood framing, drywall, plywood, insulation and CMU, and you check every count before it goes in a bid.

Which one fits you

Pick based on the job, not the brand.

Handoff probably fits better if…

  • You are a residential remodeler or builder
  • You want estimates, proposals, invoices and a client portal in one app
  • You start from a described scope rather than a plan set
  • Your plans are residential and under 7,000 sq ft

ReadMyBlueprint probably fits better if…

  • You sub drywall, framing, insulation or masonry on commercial or multifamily plan sets
  • You need counts traced to the wall they came from
  • You want to confirm every count before it goes in a bid
  • You want to test it on your own plans before paying anything

FAQ

Quick answers.

Is Handoff a good alternative to ReadMyBlueprint for commercial takeoffs?

Handoff is built for residential remodelers and builders. It produces estimates from a text, voice or photo description and adds CRM, invoicing and a client portal. Its AI plan takeoff is limited to its top, sales-led plan and to residential plans up to 7,000 sq ft. For commercial drywall, framing, insulation or masonry plan sets, ReadMyBlueprint is built to count the actual plan set.

What does ReadMyBlueprint count?

It reads the plan set and counts walls, metal and wood framing, drywall, plywood, insulation and CMU, grouped by wall type. Each wall is labeled matched, ambiguous, or needs confirmation.

Can I check the counts?

Yes. ReadMyBlueprint has a confirm step so the estimator reviews every count before it is used in a bid. On the Freeport Square test, 68.9% of walls were auto-matched and 99.8% after one Confirm all.

Does ReadMyBlueprint replace Handoff's CRM and invoicing?

No. ReadMyBlueprint is a takeoff tool only. It does not do CRM, invoicing or client portals.

Can I try ReadMyBlueprint before paying?

Yes. Your first plan set is free, no card needed. There is also a tour at www.readmyblueprint.com/tour.
