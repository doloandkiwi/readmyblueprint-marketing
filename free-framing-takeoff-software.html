Free metal stud takeoff

Stud and track counts depend on the wall type, so a count is only as good as the match to the legend: stud size and gauge, spacing, height, and the track at top and bottom. ReadMyBlueprint ties each wall to its legend entry and labels the match. Where the legend is missing a spacing or gauge, the wall is flagged instead of assumed. For one wall by hand, the free metal stud calculator needs no account, but you supply the length.

Free wood framing takeoff

Wood framing runs on studs, plates and sheathing by wall. The first plan set covers wood framing the same way: counts by wall type, each traced to the sheet it came from. The free wood stud calculator is the no-account option for a single wall.

Proof, with its limits

On a real 21-page apartment set (Freeport Square), 68.9% of walls were auto-matched to the plan's own wall legend and 99.8% after one "Confirm all". That is one plan set. The legend shares a sheet with the door schedule, so the extracted text came out scrambled and the rest was flagged for a person to confirm. Read the full test.

What the free first plan set includes and what it does not

  • Included: one plan set per account, full auto-detect, CSV and Excel export with a label column (matched, ambiguous, needs confirmation) and the sheets each count came from.
  • Not included: labor rates, regional costs, electrical, plumbing, mechanical or roofing.
  • Still yours: confirming flagged walls and verifying the counts before the bid goes out. It is assisted takeoff.
  • Checking: three independent automated passes, each checked against the others. Scanned plans are counted too.
  • After the first one: $49 per plan set, or Solo at $199 a month, or Small Firm at $499 a month, as on pricing.
