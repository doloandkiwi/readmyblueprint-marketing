Run more bids a week without hiring, or burning out.

Auto-detect handles the five biggest material categories on every plan set you upload, labeled and ready to review — so your time goes to the bid itself, not counting studs by hand at 9pm.

The problem this solves

Volume is the job, and manual takeoff doesn't scale with it.

Running a one-person or small shop means the counting and the bidding both land on you. Every hour spent manually measuring walls and cross-referencing a legend is an hour not spent on the parts of the bid that actually need your judgment — pricing, scope, relationships. And the volume only goes up when business is good, which is exactly when you have the least spare time to keep up manually.

ReadMyBlueprint measures the full plan set at once — metal framing, wood framing, drywall, insulation, CMU — and hands back a labeled dashboard in minutes instead of hours, so a plan set that used to eat an afternoon takes the time it takes to read the results.
