Questions estimators ask

Before you send a plan set

What does "first plan set free" mean?

Your first full plan set is on us. No card and no contract. If it doesn't save you time, you've lost nothing.

How fast do I get the takeoff back?

Within 24 hours of receiving the plan set, usually sooner. If the drawings are missing a wall-type schedule, we'll tell you what we need.

Do you price the job?

No. You get quantities only, so you can apply your own material and labor pricing.

What do I need to send?

A digital (vector) PDF plan set, the kind architects export. Scanned paper sets can work, but the counts are best on digital PDFs.

Which trades does it count?

Metal and wood studs and track, drywall sheets, insulation and CMU block, by wall type.

How do I know the numbers are right?

Every quantity links back to the wall and the sheet it came from, and anything the software isn't sure about is flagged for you to confirm.
