Before you try it

Straight answers.

Will it work on my plans?

If your PDF was exported from CAD (not scanned or photographed), it can be measured. Walls that match the plan's own wall-type legend are counted automatically; anything else is flagged for you to confirm.

How accurate is it?

We don't publish a magic accuracy number. On a real 21-page apartment set it matched about half the wall footage on its own and handed back a short list to confirm. Read the full test, including what it couldn't resolve.

Does it replace PlanSwift or Bluebeam?

No. It does the counting pass for framing, drywall, insulation and CMU, and exports to whatever you already use.
