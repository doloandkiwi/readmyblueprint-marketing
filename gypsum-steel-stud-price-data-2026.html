Steel Stud and Gypsum Price Data 2026: BLS PPI and Tariff Timeline

By The ReadMyBlueprint Team · Nov 25, 2026 · Updated Nov 25, 2026

Through October 2026, the BLS producer price index for steel mill products is up 32.9% from a year earlier, while the index for gypsum building materials is down 1.1%. Steel mill products moved from 300.665 in October 2025 to 399.645 in October 2026 (preliminary), and it has risen every month since a low of 291.556 in November 2025, up 37.1% from that low. Gypsum building materials moved from 368.756 to 364.838 (preliminary).

This page collects the Bureau of Labor Statistics PPI series for gypsum and steel, a timeline of the 2025 and 2026 Section 232 steel tariff actions with links to the primary documents, and notes on what the numbers do and do not show. Section 232 steel tariff timeline

  • June 4, 2025: the Section 232 tariff on steel and aluminum imports rose from 25% to 50%, per the White House fact sheet dated June 3, 2025.
  • April 6, 2026: a proclamation dated April 2, 2026 took effect, applying Section 232 duties to the full customs value of covered steel, aluminum and copper products instead of only the metal content. It sets a 50% base rate for Annex I-A steel articles and certain derivatives and 25% for Annex I-B derivatives, with lower rates for UK-melted and US-melted steel, and ends the earlier derivative inclusions process in favor of rolling additions. Source: whitehouse.gov proclamation text.
  • June 8, 2026: Proclamation 11032, issued June 1, 2026, took effect. It added four HTSUS classifications, lowered rates on a list of mostly machinery and vehicle derivatives, and cut the US-metal share needed to count as made entirely from American metal from 95% to 85%. Source: CBP guidance CSMS 68855869.

Whether a given steel stud, track or other framing product falls under one of these annexes depends on its HTS classification and country of origin. We did not check which annex any specific framing product falls under.

How to read this: the PPI measures what producers receive, not what a distributor charges a contractor, and these series cover broad steel and gypsum categories, not studs or board by size. The timing of the tariff actions and the rise in steel mill products overlap, but this page does not claim that one caused the other.

What we did not verify

  • A stud-specific price series. We did not find a BLS series for cold-formed steel studs and track, so steel mill products and iron and steel are shown as the closest published measures.
  • Distributor selling prices. Not covered by the PPI and not checked.

Frequently asked questions

How much have steel mill product prices risen in 2026?

The BLS producer price index for steel mill products (WPU1017) was 399.645 in October 2026 (preliminary) against 300.665 in October 2025, a rise of 32.9%. It was 315.369 in January 2026, so it is up 26.7% since the start of the year.

Have gypsum wallboard prices gone up in 2026?

Not in the BLS producer price index for gypsum building materials (WPU13710102), which was 364.838 in October 2026 (preliminary) against 368.756 a year earlier, a 1.1% decline. Distributor-posted manufacturer price notices exist, but we did not verify them against the manufacturers' own letters.

What is the Section 232 tariff on steel in 2026?

A proclamation dated April 2, 2026, effective April 6, 2026, sets a 50% base rate on Annex I-A steel articles and certain derivatives and 25% on Annex I-B derivatives, applied to the full customs value, with lower rates for UK-melted and US-melted steel. Proclamation 11032 amended it effective June 8, 2026. Check the HTS classification of the product.

Are steel studs covered by the steel tariffs?

It depends on the product's HTS classification, which annex it falls under and where it was made. We did not check the classification of any specific stud or track product, so confirm with your supplier or a customs broker.

What BLS series track steel and gypsum prices?

WPU1017 (steel mill products), WPU101 (iron and steel), WPU13710102 (gypsum building materials) and PCU327420327420 (gypsum product manufacturing). All are published by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics and not seasonally adjusted.
