Questions

Common questions

Is there a PlanSwift alternative for Mac?

Yes. ReadMyBlueprint runs in any browser, so it works on a Mac with nothing to install.

How is ReadMyBlueprint different from PlanSwift?

With PlanSwift you measure each wall by hand. ReadMyBlueprint measures walls from the PDF's vector geometry, matches them to your wall legend, and flags anything it isn't sure of.

Does ReadMyBlueprint work on scanned plans?

No. It needs vector PDFs exported from CAD or Revit, because it measures the real drawing lines. On a scanned page it tells you it can't measure it instead of guessing.
