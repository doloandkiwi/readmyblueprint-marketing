How it works

An online takeoff in three steps

1. Upload the PDF

Use the plan set your architect exported from CAD or Revit. Every page is scanned automatically, and each page's printed scale is read for you.

2. Check the flagged walls

Walls that match a spec on your drawings are ready. Anything ambiguous or unmatched is flagged, with the plan's own material pre-filled where possible. One "Confirm all" usually finishes it.

3. Export to your bid

Download the material list as CSV or Excel. Add doors, windows and fixtures with manual count.

What you get back

  • Metal studs and track
  • Wood framing and sheathing
  • Drywall sheets
  • Insulation coverage
  • CMU block counts

Questions

Common questions

What is online takeoff software?

Online (or cloud) takeoff software runs in a web browser. You upload a PDF plan set and measure or count quantities without installing anything, and it works on Windows or Mac.

Is ReadMyBlueprint online takeoff software?

Yes. It runs in your browser at app.readmyblueprint.com. Upload a CAD-exported PDF and it automatically counts studs, track, drywall sheets, insulation and CMU block, and you can export the list to CSV or Excel.

Which takeoff software is desktop-only?

PlanSwift and On-Screen Takeoff are Windows desktop programs you install. STACK, Square Takeoff, zzTakeoff and ReadMyBlueprint run in a browser.

How much does online takeoff software cost?

Published comparisons put most online takeoff tools at roughly $150 to $250 per user per month. ReadMyBlueprint charges per plan set instead: $49 per plan set, $15 for your first one, or $199 a month for 10 plan sets.

Can I do a takeoff online without a subscription?
