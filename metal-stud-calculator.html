Metal stud & track calculator

Enter the wall length and spacing to get stud and track counts for a steel-framed wall, the same math ReadMyBlueprint uses on a full plan set.

Studs = wall length ÷ spacing + 1 (one extra to close the run). Track runs top and bottom, so it's twice the wall length in 10 ft sticks. Add studs at every corner, opening and intersection; this is a straight-run starting point.

Questions

Common questions

How many metal studs do I need per foot of wall?

At 16" on center it's 0.75 studs per linear foot plus one to close each run, so a 100 ft run needs 76 studs before corners, openings and waste. At 24" on center it's 0.5 per foot.

How much track do I need?

Track runs along the floor and the ceiling, so you need twice the wall length. 100 ft of wall needs 200 ft of track: 20 ten-foot sticks before waste.

What waste factor should I use for steel framing?

5–10% is common for straight runs; go higher for walls with many openings, corners or short segments.
