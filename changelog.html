● Changelog

What's shipped

A real, dated log of what's changed — no marketing spin, just what's new and why it matters for your bids.

Sep 10, 2026

Faster, cleaner, and mobile-friendly

  • The plan workspace now works properly on a phone — review results and add anything auto-detect missed from the jobsite, not just a desktop.
  • Routine security and dependency updates across the platform.
Sep 9, 2026

First-time offer, a real getting-started guide, and a clearer results view

  • New accounts can try their first plan set for $15 instead of $49 — once per account.
  • Added a full step-by-step guide, right on the page, covering upload through export.
  • Auto-detect now shows a live progress estimate instead of a bare "reading plan…" spinner.
  • Results are now a real category grid — Metal, Lumber, Drywall, Insulation, CMU — instead of one mixed list, with a one-click CSV export straight from the results.
  • Unspecified wall thicknesses are grouped sensibly instead of listing dozens of near-duplicate rows for normal measurement noise.
Sep 8, 2026

Public launch: accounts, billing, and full auto-detection

  • Real accounts with private, isolated data per customer — your plans are never visible to anyone else.
  • Subscriptions and one-time uploads, backed by a 7-day money-back guarantee.
  • Full-plan-set auto-detection shipped for the first time: metal framing, wood framing, drywall, plywood, insulation, and CMU block, read directly from your drawing's own geometry and wall-type legend.
  • Password reset and email notifications added.
  • Live error monitoring and uptime checks turned on across the whole platform.
Aug 23, 2026

Core takeoff engine hardened against real-world plan sets

  • Fixed a measurement bug affecting some rotated large-format sheets (common on D/E-size architectural and civil drawings) — every affected page re-verified against hand-calculated values after the fix, with zero regressions.
  • Added a coverage check that tells you when a page couldn't be fully read, instead of silently returning a partial result.
