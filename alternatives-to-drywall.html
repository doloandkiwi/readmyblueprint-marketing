Alternatives to drywall: what each one changes in your takeoff

By The ReadMyBlueprint Team · Nov 28, 2026

The common alternatives to drywall on a plan set are veneer plaster, cement board, FRP panels, wood or plywood paneling, and CMU. Each changes the unit you count: board in sheets, plaster by area, panels in pieces, block in units. Some change only the finish layer; CMU replaces the whole wall, framing included.

Which alternatives actually show up on plans?

MaterialWhere you see itWhat you count
Veneer plasterHigh-end interiors, restorationBase board sheets, plus plaster by area
Cement boardTile walls, showers, wet areasSheets (3x5 is common), screws, mesh tape
FRP panelsKitchens, restrooms, janitor roomsPanels, trim pieces, adhesive
Plywood or panelingGarages, utility rooms, feature wallsSheets or boards, plus waste
CMUStairs, shafts, demising wallsBlock units, grout, rebar, lintels

Is it replacing the board, or going on top of it?

Read this first, because it decides whether your drywall count changes at all.

  • On top: FRP is commonly glued over gypsum board, and paneling may go over it too. Your board count stays; the finish is an added line, and the finish level on that face may drop.
  • Instead of: cement board in a shower replaces gypsum board on that face. Deduct the area from your board count and add it as its own line.
  • Different base: veneer plaster goes over a special gypsum base (often called blueboard), so the sheet count stays but the product and the finishing scope change.
  • Different wall: CMU replaces studs and board entirely, unless it gets furring and board on one face.

The wall type legend and the finish schedule tell you which. When they disagree, ask before you bid and note it.

Worked example: one restroom wall

A 12 ft restroom wall, 9 ft high, metal studs, 5/8 in. board both sides. On the restroom face, tile runs to 7 ft over cement board, and FRP covers the 2 ft above it over gypsum board.

  • Corridor face: 12 × 9 = 108 sq ft of gypsum board.
  • Restroom face, cement board: 12 × 7 = 84 sq ft. At 15 sq ft per 3x5 sheet that is 5.6, so 6 sheets before waste.
  • Restroom face, gypsum board above the tile: 12 × 2 = 24 sq ft.
  • FRP: 24 sq ft, less than one 4x8 panel by area; order by the panel layout, plus trim.

Gypsum board on this wall is 132 sq ft, not the 216 sq ft a "board both sides" count gives. That 84 sq ft is cement board you either carry or exclude, and your bid should say which.

How is CMU counted?

By units. A standard 8x8x16 block lays up as an 8 by 16 in. face including the mortar joint, so it covers 0.889 sq ft and you need 1.125 blocks per sq ft of wall. Grout, rebar, bond beam and lintel block come from the structural details. The CMU takeoff guide and block count guide go further. On a mixed floor, keep block on its own line so it never gets buried inside a drywall area total.

Does a different finish affect a fire rating?

It can. A rating belongs to a tested assembly, and the Gypsum Association's GA-600 manual is one common reference for those assemblies. Swapping or adding layers on a rated wall is the designer's call, not the estimator's. Count what the legend specifies; if a finish note seems to conflict with a rated assembly, send an RFI. The fire-rated wall assembly chart covers how ratings show on the legend.

Is plaster a drywall bid or a separate one?

Usually separate. Veneer plaster is hand-troweled in one or two coats over its gypsum base, and fewer crews do it. If you only bid board, count the base sheets and list plaster as an exclusion. See plaster takeoff for that trade's side.

Where does ReadMyBlueprint fit?

FAQ

Does FRP replace drywall?

Usually not; it is commonly installed over gypsum board or masonry. Check the finish schedule and the detail.

What board goes behind tile in a shower?

Cement board or another tile backer the spec names. Deduct that area from your gypsum board count.

How many CMU blocks per square foot?

1.125 for standard 8x8x16 block, before openings and waste.
