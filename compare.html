ReadMyBlueprint vs. STACK & Bluebeam

STACK and Bluebeam are real, established platforms — this isn't a "we're better at everything" page. It's an honest look at where each one actually fits, so you don't buy more (or less) than you need.

The short version

Same job, different scope.

STACK and Bluebeam are full preconstruction-and-field platforms: takeoff, estimating, project management, field collaboration, mobile apps, regional cost data, and more, priced and sold accordingly. ReadMyBlueprint does one job — turning a real digital plan set into a labeled material count — and is priced for a solo estimator or small shop instead of an enterprise rollout.

Which one fits you

Pick based on the job, not the brand.

STACK or Bluebeam probably fits better if…

  • You need field collaboration, daily reports, or RFIs — not just takeoff
  • You're outfitting a multi-person team with shared seats and admin controls
  • You want a native mobile app for jobsite crews
  • You need built-in regional material/labor cost data

ReadMyBlueprint probably fits better if…

  • You just need a fast, trustworthy material count from a real plan set
  • You want to see the real price and start today, with no sales call
  • You're a solo estimator, small shop, or a homeowner doing one project
  • You want to try it for $15 before deciding on anything bigger
