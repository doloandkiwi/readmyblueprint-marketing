AI takeoff software for drywall, framing and CMU

Short answer: AI takeoff software reads your PDF plan set and counts materials automatically. ReadMyBlueprint does this for wall trades: upload a CAD-exported PDF and get studs, track, drywall sheets, insulation and CMU block back in minutes, each number traced to the wall it came from. It costs $49 per plan set with no subscription, and your first plan set is $15.

How it works

What "AI takeoff" actually does

Manual digital takeoff means clicking every wall, corner and opening on a PDF. AI takeoff does the measuring pass for you, and you spend your time checking the result instead of producing it.

What ReadMyBlueprint does

  • Reads the real vector geometry in your PDF, so lengths come from the drawing, not from a guess about the drawing
  • Reads each page's printed scale on its own
  • Matches wall thicknesses to your plan's wall-type legend to pick the right assembly
  • Labels every quantity: matched, ambiguous, or needs confirmation
  • Exports a CSV/Excel material list for your bid

What it doesn't do

  • Scanned or photographed plans. It needs a CAD-exported PDF.
  • MEP, concrete, roofing or sitework. It covers walls: framing, drywall, insulation and CMU.
  • Doors, windows and fixtures automatically. You add those with manual count.
  • Pricing and labor. It gives you quantities; your bid is still yours.

Questions

Common questions

What is AI takeoff software?

AI takeoff software reads a digital plan set and measures or counts quantities automatically, instead of an estimator clicking every wall and corner by hand. You review the result, fix what it flagged, and export the quantities into your bid.

How does ReadMyBlueprint's AI takeoff work?

It reads the vector geometry inside a CAD-exported PDF, measures every wall, and matches each wall to the plan's own wall-type legend. It then returns studs, track, drywall sheets, insulation and CMU block, with every quantity labeled matched, ambiguous or needs-confirmation.

How much does AI takeoff software cost?

As of September 2026, Togal.AI lists $299 per user per month billed yearly (about $3,588 a year). ReadMyBlueprint is $49 per plan set with no subscription, $199 a month for 10 plan sets, and your first plan set is $15.

Is AI takeoff accurate?

It depends on the plan set. A good AI takeoff tool should show you which numbers it is sure of and which it guessed. ReadMyBlueprint never folds a guess into your total: anything it can't match to a spec on your drawings is flagged for you to confirm.

Does AI takeoff work on scanned plans?

ReadMyBlueprint does not. It needs vector PDFs exported from CAD or Revit. On a scanned page it tells you it can't measure it instead of guessing.
